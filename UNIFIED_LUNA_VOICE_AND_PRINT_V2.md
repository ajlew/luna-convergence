# Unified Luna Voice and Print v2

The clean daily experience is now the design and language master for
daily, monthly and yearly readings.

## Shared visible language

- Luna says
- Do
- Don't
- Your move
- Why Luna sees this
- Solar Convergence
- Key dates and planetary timing
- Full technical evidence

## Agency language

The longer passive sentence was removed.

> Shift from applicant to gatekeeper.

> Attention opens the door. Effort decides what stays.

## Monthly restraint

Removed from the visible monthly page:

- Concrete possibilities—not promises
- scenario chips
- You remain the main character
- a separate convergence line in the black hero
- the second Luna-says paragraph
- three repeated chapter-level Your Move blocks
- the large validation quotation

Concrete examples now sit inside the story. The explanatory disclaimer
appears only in the footer.

## Print controls

Monthly and yearly pages now provide:

- A4 or A3
- Portrait or Landscape
- Include evidence
- Print or save report

Print CSS changes the grid to suit the selected orientation. Portrait
uses a single-column reading flow; landscape can retain wider chapter
grids. Closed evidence remains excluded unless selected.
