# Luna v3.26 — Concentration Themes + Solar Clock Masthead

This release preserves the v3.25 Daily, Monthly, Natal, Solar Year, Stripe fulfilment,
PDF/email delivery, historical ephemeris memory and evidence layers.

## New: concentration theme synthesis

Luna now distinguishes the forest from the trees.

- **Natal:** a compact `Chart emphasis · the forest` line detects repeated sign environments
  (for example Sun + Mercury in Sagittarius, Moon + Saturn in Pisces, Venus + Mars in Capricorn).
  This is a whole-chart synthesis only; `Your strongest signatures` remains the behavioural
  aspect layer, so the same Sun–Saturn aspect is not interpreted twice.
- **Monthly:** `Where the sky is gathering` detects the strongest persistent same-sign cluster
  during the month, weights personal/social planets ahead of generational background pairs,
  maps the sign to the customer's whole-sign house, and turns it into one overriding story.
- **Paid Monthly:** the hierarchy is now current-sky concentration → natal overlay → chronology.
  The natal overlay and PDF/email fulfilment remain unchanged.

For Sagittarius in August 2026 the engine identifies Sun + Mercury + Jupiter in Leo as the
strongest monthly field and maps it to house 9 (travel, publishing, law, education and foreign markets).

## Solar wave across the site

The solar-year wave is now shared site chrome:

- Daily/Home, the legacy August ad alias, and Solar Year retain the full labelled clock.
- Other customer-facing pages receive a compact 72px version with the same real solar curve,
  four gate marks and current-Sun marker, but without repeated tropic/equinox labels.
- Operational pages (`ephemeris-admin`, `editorial-preview`, `payment-success`) remain clear.

This keeps the solar clock as a Luna brand signature without turning every page into an astronomy panel.
