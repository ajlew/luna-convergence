# Luna v3.52 — Birthday Card Keepsake Themes

The Birthday Card page now offers two customer-selectable designs on the same page:

- **Midnight Painted** — warm-white Luna typography over hand-painted blue paper, with a sparse star field.
- **Ivory Letter** — dark Luna typography over tactile ivory paper.

Both designs preserve the same:

- calculated Sun and Moon placements;
- Weekly Studio-style aspect evidence;
- live validated Luna poem;
- 1080 × 1920 PNG;
- matching one-page 9:16 PDF;
- Luna fonts and logo.

The selected style is stored on the generated card and applied identically to both downloads. No alternate page, duplicate calculation path, canned poem or astrology fallback was added.
