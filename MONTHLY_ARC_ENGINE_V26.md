# Monthly Arc Engine v2.6

## Purpose

The monthly report no longer chooses its main story from the two largest raw
house totals. It now builds a chronological path through the strongest timed
convergences.

## Equation

```text
Inherited state
+ trigger hierarchy
+ house / planet / ruler convergence
+ ranked scenario families
+ good-bad tension
+ temporal narrative roles
= monthly arc
```

The score is an internal symbolic support model. It is not an empirical
probability and does not claim that planets physically cause the event.

## Trigger hierarchy

The engine gives more narrative weight to:

1. eclipses;
2. New Moons and Full Moons;
3. planetary stations;
4. exact major aspects;
5. ingresses;
6. slower background contacts.

A native sign ruler receives an additional multiplier. For Sagittarius,
Jupiter therefore carries extra narrative weight.

## Carryover

The engine reviews the seven days before the month starts. A lunation, eclipse,
station or major aspect can establish the inherited condition that opens the
new month.

## Scenario families

The engine ranks event families such as:

- unexpected cost or disappointing amount;
- grant, inheritance or insurance money;
- mortgage, loan or formal funding;
- paperwork and verification;
- publishing or audience response;
- visa, legal or education progress;
- travel;
- career interview or offer;
- relationship opening;
- property or home decision;
- contracts and agreements.

Each family retains the supporting dates, houses and planets used by the score.

## Narrative roles

Every selected convergence receives a role:

- inherited state;
- inciting event;
- complication;
- pivot;
- climax;
- resolution.

The visible monthly report converts these into three customer chapters while
the evidence dropdown preserves the complete six-role trail.

## Customer interface

`Why Luna sees this` now includes:

- the arc equation;
- six narrative-role cards;
- ranked scenario families;
- possible manifestations;
