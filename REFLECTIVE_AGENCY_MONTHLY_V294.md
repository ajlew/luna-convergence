# Reflective Agency Monthly Voice v2.9.4

## Purpose

Keep Luna active without starting every sentence with **you**. Agency now comes from verbs, movement and selection rather than repeated direct address.

## Four-act language

1. **Shaping possibility into momentum**
2. **Bringing details into focus**
3. **Watching whether the spark holds**
4. **Selecting what remains in life**

## Voice rule

Use second person where emotional intimacy matters. Elsewhere prefer:

- gerund-led headings;
- scene-led sentences;
- clear active verbs;
- one direct instruction rather than several sentences beginning with “you.”

## Result

The August Sagittarius customer reading now uses four second-person references across roughly 640 customer-facing words, while preserving the signature line:

> From reading the future to writing it.
