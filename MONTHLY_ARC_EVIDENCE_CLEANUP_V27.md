# Monthly Arc Evidence Cleanup v2.7

## Why the section was removed

The first customer pages already explain July through:

- the four-paragraph Luna story;
- the three monthly chapters;
- Dates worth circling;
- Love, Work and Money;
- the final action plan.

The former **Monthly arc equation** and six narrative-role cards repeated those
same events inside **Why Luna sees this**. Because all dropdowns open for print,
the repetition consumed nearly a full printed page without adding customer value.

## New Why Luna sees this structure

The section now contains only:

1. central storyline;
2. theme;
3. convergence;
4. a three-line evidence path:
   - starting condition;
   - midmonth test;
   - release and result;
5. the Luna validation rule.

The arc equation and detailed role summaries remain in the calculation engine but
are no longer rendered as customer copy.

## Scenario families

The ranked scenario table remains available under **Full technical evidence**.
This keeps the evidence traceable without repeating the monthly story in two
places.

## Date format

All customer-facing monthly and yearly dates now use day-month-year order:

```text
1 July 2026
1-10 July 2026
28 June-2 July 2026
```

ISO storage values such as `2026-07-01` remain unchanged inside the calculation
data and order references.
