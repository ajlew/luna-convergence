# Luna Shorts Playbook v1

## Purpose

Each Luna Short should do one job: take one meaningful sky event and turn it into one human consequence, then send the viewer to Luna to choose their star sign.

The structure is adapted from the two supplied short astrology videos: both open with the date, identify one primary planetary pattern, translate it quickly into ordinary life, then invite the viewer to go deeper by sign.

## 35–55 second structure

### 0–4 sec — Date + hook

Open with the date and the consequence, not a greeting.

**Template**

> Saturday 15 August. One conversation or idea can grow much faster than usual today.

### 4–12 sec — The sky signal

Name no more than one primary aspect or combination.

**Template**

> Mercury meets Jupiter in Leo: communication meets expansion in a sign that wants expression and visibility.

Avoid lists of degrees, minor aspects or house jargon here.

### 12–28 sec — Human translation

Explain what someone could notice in ordinary life.

Use two or three concrete domains at most:

- conversation / decision;
- work / opportunity;
- relationship / visibility;
- money / value;
- travel / learning.

### 28–38 sec — The counterweight

If there is a genuine pressure aspect, give it one sentence. No warning without a move.

**Template**

> The opportunity is real, but bigger is not automatically better. Check the facts before promising more than the situation can carry.

### 38–47 sec — Personalisation bridge

This is Luna's differentiator.

**Template**

> Where this lands depends on your sign. Leo is not the same house for Sagittarius as it is for Cancer or Capricorn.

### 47–55 sec — Single CTA

**Default CTA**

> Choose your star sign at Luna Convergence. Today's reading is free.

Do not stack calls to action. One Short = one destination.

## Luna voice rules

1. One sky event.
2. One consequence.
3. One useful move.
4. One invitation to personalise by sign.
5. Aim for 80–120 spoken words.
6. Use captions on screen.
7. Use the aspect name once; translate it immediately.
8. Do not explain the calculation engine in the Short.
9. Do not make deterministic claims about what will happen.
10. End on the customer's next action, not on Luna's credentials.

## Visual structure

**Format:** vertical 9:16.

- 0–4 sec: date + 4–7 word hook.
- 4–12 sec: planet symbols/names with one aspect label.
- 12–38 sec: narrator or minimal animated typography.
- 38–47 sec: twelve-sign strip or fast sign-switch visual from Luna.
- 47–55 sec: Luna logo + `Choose your star sign`.

The fast Daily sign dropdown is useful footage because it demonstrates that the interpretation changes immediately by sign.

## Example: Mercury + Jupiter

**Hook:** `An idea can get bigger today.`

**Signal:** Mercury meets Jupiter in Leo.

**Translation:** speaking, pitching, publishing, learning or showing a talent can gain momentum.

**Counterweight:** expansion needs proof; do not confuse confidence with certainty.

**Bridge:** the life area changes by zodiac sign.

**CTA:** choose your sign on Luna for today's free reading.

## Example: Mercury + Chiron

**Hook:** `Say it — but notice what makes you hesitate.`

**Signal:** Mercury in Leo clashes with Chiron.

**Translation:** an idea may be ready to be expressed while self-doubt, value or old sensitivity makes the delivery harder.

**Move:** separate the quality of the idea from the fear of how it will be received.

**Bridge + CTA:** choose your sign on Luna to see where the tension lands today.

## Production workflow

1. Ask Luna for the strongest non-lunar daily signal.
2. Verify the aspect/date in the ephemeris engine.
3. Draft the Short with the six-part structure above.
4. Cut anything that does not change the viewer's decision.
5. Record vertical video.
6. Add burned-in captions.
7. Show the Luna Daily sign selector for 2–4 seconds.
8. Link directly to the Daily landing page.
9. Use one campaign-tagged URL when advertising so traffic can be separated from organic YouTube visits.
