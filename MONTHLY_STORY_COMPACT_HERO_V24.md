# Monthly Story + Compact Hero v2.4

## Product rule

Daily readings stay punchy. Monthly and year-ahead reports must provide
enough narrative, timing and practical interpretation to justify a paid
product.

## Compact hero

The black opening now contains only:

- Monthly / sign / period
- emotional hook
- one-line serious theme

The full Luna narrative, Do / Don't and action guidance begin on white.
This removes the oversized black block visible in the earlier preview.

## Monthly story

The customer-facing story now moves chronologically through four stages:

1. the opening possibility;
2. early attention and invitations;
3. the central turning point and practical complication;
4. the final placement test.

The Sagittarius August example includes recognisable possibilities such
as a message, flirtation, trip, course, creative launch, publishing
opportunity, audience response, shared cost, distance and career/home
consequences. These remain possibilities rather than guarantees.

## Visible value

The page now includes:

- four-paragraph Luna story;
- three detailed monthly acts;
- four Dates worth circling cards;
- romance active / romance quiet;
- Love / Work / Money outcomes;
- one final action plan;
- expandable evidence, automatically opened for printing.

## Print control

The print toolbar no longer floats over the reading. It appears after the
report footer, so it cannot cover chapter text while scrolling or during
screenshots.
