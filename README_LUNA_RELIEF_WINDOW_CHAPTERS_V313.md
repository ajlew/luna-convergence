# Luna Monthly v3.13 — Relief + Window Fidelity

This update preserves the v3.12 trajectory engine and tightens the customer-facing handoff.

## Changes

- Customer-facing `Countercurrent` is now **Relief**. The internal `COUNTERCURRENT` role remains available for backwards compatibility.
- One trajectory window renders as one customer chapter with one Luna move.
- A displayed event must fall inside its displayed influence window.
- September 2026 restores the Full Moon in Aries as the late-month result and anchors the 16–18 September relationship test to Venus square Pluto.
- Verified bridges are explained in plain language with nearby astronomical movements.
- Timed strategies now name the life area that is being negotiated, advanced or questioned.
- Existing Daily, Monthly, Yearly, payment, print, ephemeris-admin, scenario and technical-evidence functionality is preserved.
