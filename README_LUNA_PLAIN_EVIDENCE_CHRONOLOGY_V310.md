# Luna v3.10 — Plain Evidence + Single Chronology

This build is additive and preserves the astronomy, convergence, decision, romance, payment, print, ephemeris-admin, historical-test, daily and yearly systems.

## Customer-facing changes

- **How [Month] Unfolds is now the one authoritative chronology.** The duplicate `Dates worth circling` and duplicated `Key dates and planetary timing` prose renderers are removed from the customer report; the underlying `key_dates` data remains available to the engine.
- Each chronology beat now keeps the astronomy beside the interpretation: exact event, influence window, plain-English life area, verified concentration counts, named aspects/orbs, and the strategy for that beat.
- Luna no longer hides a verified cluster behind phrases such as “several signals.” If five planets are involved in three exact pressure contacts, the report says so and names the contacts.
- Internal labels such as `H10`, `H5`, or `House 11` are translated into life areas throughout the customer report and customer-visible evidence tables.
- Whole-sign mapping is explained in ordinary language, e.g. “For Sagittarius, Virgo describes career, reputation, authority and visible results.”
- The Solar Background is also translated into life-area language.
- When a bridge and resolution use the same astronomical event, Luna tells the story once rather than duplicating the event card.

## First-principles contract v1.2

New permanent rules:

1. Customer-facing astrology uses plain life areas, not internal house identifiers.
2. When a pattern is verified, state it plainly: give the count, planets and aspect evidence close to the claim.
3. One customer chronology is authoritative; duplicate date-by-date retellings are compressed into that chronology.

## Regression example: Sagittarius September 2017

The mid-month Virgo career window now explicitly exposes the verified build-up, including the five-planet / three-pressure-contact pattern and the exact Sun–Saturn, Mercury–Mars and Mercury–Neptune contacts calculated by the engine. Nothing is hard-coded for Sagittarius 2017; the wording is derived from the normal event data.
