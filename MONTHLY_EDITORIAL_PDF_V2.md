# Monthly Editorial PDF v2

The monthly report no longer uses the generic Markdown-to-PDF layout for its
customer pages. It now has a dedicated editorial renderer.

## Customer hierarchy

```text
Emotional hook
-> serious calculated theme
-> concise story
-> Do / Don't
-> three monthly chapters
-> love, work and money
-> strategy and key dates
-> Solar Convergence
-> Explainable Evidence
-> technical appendix
```

## Visual changes

- full-black editorial cover rather than a black rectangle inside a white page;
- the convergence appears before the headline;
- the witty hook and serious theme are visually separate;
- page 2 uses a magazine-style two-column layout;
- three chapter cards replace long continuous prose;
- love, work and money use distinct editorial hooks;
- key dates use readable cards instead of a four-column spreadsheet;
- Solar Convergence has its own designed evidence page;
- the Sky Snapshot uses cards rather than one large grey table;
- tables are reserved mainly for the technical appendix.

No planetary calculations, houses, transitions, Solar Convergence values,
Stripe references or order-capture behavior were changed.
