# Luna v3.27 — Sign-Specific Monthly Chronology

This release keeps the v3.26 page architecture and functionality intact while strengthening the public **How [Month] unfolds** narrative.

## What changed

- Astronomy, dates, event anchors, influence windows, evidence accordions, paid Natal overlay, Stripe flow, PDFs, email fulfilment, Daily, Natal, House Guide, Solar Year, historical astronomy memory and the compact solar-wave masthead remain intact.
- The three public monthly chapters now have distinct jobs: **opening evidence → reset/commitment → consequence**.
- Chapter headlines and moves are generated from the activated life area rather than a shared generic template.
- Internal support/friction scores remain available to the engine and technical evidence but no longer leak into repeated customer prose.
- The strongest August examples now read distinctly:
  - Pisces: **A workable routine begins to prove itself → The routine stops being temporary → The cost of the routine becomes personal**.
  - Capricorn: **A financial or trust issue becomes easier to define → The real price of the agreement becomes visible → What was financial now forces a decision**.
  - Sagittarius: **The wider road gets its first piece of proof → The wider road demands a real commitment → The wider road reaches your front door**.
- Secondary countercurrents such as Sagittarius romance/creative relief are preserved.

## Editorial rule

The same astronomical event may be shared by all signs, but the customer-facing dateline must explain what that event **does in the sign's activated life area** and how it changes the story established by the previous chapter.
