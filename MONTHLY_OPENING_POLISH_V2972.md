# Luna Convergence v2.9.7.2 - Monthly Opening Polish

This focused release refines the opening customer section without changing the Universal Monthly Evidence Engine, Daily calculations, payment flow, print isolation, or Luna's overall visual structure.

## Changes

- Reduced the web `Luna Says` type scale so it reads as editorial introduction rather than a second hero headline.
- Widened the desktop text measure and added justified alignment with hyphenation.
- Preserved left alignment on screens under 720px for clean mobile reading.
- Kept the complete opening block together during printing where space permits.
- Replaced the mechanical house-label paragraph with a shorter two-sentence customer translation.
- Replaced the long sequence sentence with three short editorial beats.
- Removed repeated house terminology from the PDF `Your month at a glance` copy.
- Reordered the final three moves as identify, clarify, act.
- Preserved A4 searchable PDFs, isolated print windows, evidence traceability, Yearly hidden status, and private Monthly preview.

## Aries example

**Luna Says**

> The month opens through a creative, romantic or entrepreneurial opening. As it develops, attention shifts toward the rhythm that could help it grow in real life.

**Sequence line**

> Momentum builds first. Meaning sharpens next. By late month, the strongest possibility has found a workable form.

**Three moves**

1. Name the spark worth developing.
2. Give the strongest spark one clear next stage.
3. Create the rhythm that can carry the result beyond the month.
