# Luna v3.9 — Narrative Progression + Strategy Authority

This update is additive. It preserves the existing ephemeris, convergence, scenario,
payment, preview, historical-test, print/PDF, daily, monthly and yearly pathways.

## Permanent first-principles additions

- Evidence may recur; interpretation must advance.
- A scenario manifestation is stated once; later narrative roles must deepen, connect or resolve it.
- Choice preserves domain asymmetry before the month is synthesised.
- The narrator may explain a strategy but cannot issue advice that contradicts it.

## Monthly changes

1. Repetitive manifestation lists are compressed into progression: opening -> concrete terms -> bridge/complication -> consequence -> decision.
2. Public date-card responses are rewritten only after the Truth Gate/domain strategy is calculated.
3. A portfolio strategy sits above the domain truth gates: FULL ADVANCE, SELECTIVE ADVANCE, PROBE, RENEGOTIATE, DEFENSIVE HOLD or PASS.
4. Work/Love/Money cards inherit the relevant narrative house before falling back to their strongest background house.
5. Every public narrative beat now stores direct houses, connected houses, selected narrative house and the connection reason.
6. Technical evidence includes a Narrative Evidence Ledger and plain-English decision evidence.
7. Do/Don't and Why Luna Sees This are strategy-aware.

No Sagittarius-2017 or September-2026 special case is hard-coded.
