# Luna v3.29 — Weekly View

## Public page

- Adds `/weekly-view` to the primary navigation.
- Uses the visitor's timezone and always presents Monday through Sunday.
- On Sundays, the page opens the next week rather than a week that is ending.
- Shows one evidence-led shared-sky card per day.
- Keeps the existing sign-specific Daily and Monthly engines unchanged.

## Owner production page

- Adds the hidden `/weekly-studio` route.
- Any selected date is normalised to its Monday.
- Provides seven Canva-ready text blocks with Streamlit's copy control.
- Provides a combined `.txt` download, browser print button and the exact
  1080 × 1920 Luna video background.

## Voice and evidence

Every card is generated from a calculated planetary aspect. Copy remains dark,
direct and concise, but does not promise outcomes. Repetition penalties stop a
single slow aspect from taking over the entire week.
