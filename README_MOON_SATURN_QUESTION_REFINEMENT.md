# Moon–Saturn Question Refinement

The July 30 twelve-sign review showed that the fourth reflection question
repeatedly exposed the internal phrase:

```text
reserve, consistency and emotional restraint
```

The phrase was astrologically accurate but did not sound like natural customer
language.

## Update

Moon sextile Saturn now uses twelve house-pair-specific questions. Examples:

- Aries: Who is showing through their actions that they can build this with me?
- Cancer: Can this person respect my boundaries consistently enough for trust to grow?
- Libra: What consistent action would show that the interest is mutual rather than performative?
- Pisces: Is there steady interest, or is distance allowing the fantasy to grow?

The generic Moon–Saturn fallback now uses:

```text
patient effort and dependable behaviour
```

The release test verifies that all twelve questions are unique, each sign still
receives four non-overlapping questions, and the shared evidence remains
Moon sextile Saturn.
