# Luna v3.11 — Nature-led trajectory + countercurrent

This update adds a chronological trajectory layer without removing Luna's existing ephemeris, convergence, truth-gate, payment, preview, print, historical-test, daily or yearly functionality.

## Permanent rule

Nature → Pattern → **Trajectory** → Convergence → Meaning → Choice → Experience → Learning.

The engine now asks what changes over time: what begins, builds, peaks, eases or reverses. A monthly average can no longer hide a late storm or an early relief window.

## Bridges are optional

A tertiary bridge now needs a 0.68 convergence score (previously 0.58). A weak third house remains background rather than being forced into the story. September 2017 Sagittarius therefore keeps career/work as the main pressure and friends/future plans as the later consequence; romance is not forced into a structural bridge.

## Countercurrent

A directly supported secondary area can become a **COUNTERCURRENT**: relief or support that offsets the main pressure without causing or resolving the main plot. September 2017 identifies romance/creativity as relief through supportive Uranus contacts, while the late Jupiter–Uranus opposition makes that relief less stable. The strategy is therefore enjoy/question rather than automatic advance.

## Customer report cleanup

The standalone generic `Romance and validation` block is no longer rendered. Romance remains in the Love domain row and enters `How the month unfolds` whenever Nature makes it relevant. The customer technical section also stops rendering `Ranked scenario families` and `Monthly background weight`; those data remain in the result object for QA/admin use.

`How the month unfolds` is capped at three nature-led chapters: opening signal, concentration, late result/peak. Extra internal arc roles remain in the evidence ledger but do not get to manufacture additional customer scenes.
