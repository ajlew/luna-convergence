# Active Agency Monthly Voice v2.9.3

The Monthly customer voice now keeps the reader active without turning the
report into emotional homework.

## Core rule

Luna does not position the reader as waiting for the month, a partner or an
opportunity to decide her future.

Every recommendation gives the reader an active verb:

- choose;
- shape;
- ask;
- notice;
- write;
- move;
- build.

## New customer translation

| Old language | Active customer language |
|---|---|
| proof | what their actions show |
| test | what happens next |
| responsibility | steadiness or shared expectations |
| terms | what comes with it |
| gatekeeper | author of the next move |
| practical reality | the life you are creating |

## Signature line

> From reading the future to writing it.

## Sagittarius August 2026 structure

1. You turn possibility into momentum.
2. You bring the details into focus.
3. You see whether the spark can hold.
4. You choose what belongs in your life.

The astrology and technical evidence remain unchanged. This release changes
the customer translation and action language.
