# Monthly Agency Webpage v1

## Product position

The monthly product is now a responsive Luna webpage. The browser creates
a PDF only when the reader chooses `Print or save report`.

The old ReportLab/browser-generated PDF modules remain as internal
backups, but they are no longer the primary monthly customer experience.

## Editorial rule

The reader is the protagonist. People, attention, romance, work and
opportunities enter her story and are evaluated against her standards,
choices and future.

The recurring Luna rules are:

> You are not waiting to be selected. You are deciding what deserves access.

> Attention gets your notice. Effort earns your interest. Consistency
> earns a place in your life.

## Visible monthly structure

1. Monthly sign/date/convergence
2. Witty emotional hook
3. `Luna says`
4. Do / Don't
5. Agency rule
6. Concrete real-life scenarios
7. Three-act monthly storyline
8. Romance active / romance quiet
9. Love / work / money
10. Your next move

## Closed evidence

- Why Luna sees this
- Solar Convergence
- Key dates and planetary timing
- Full technical evidence

## Printing

The page includes a native browser print control and an optional
`Include evidence in print` checkbox. Closed evidence remains excluded
from the normal print unless the reader selects it.
