# Luna v3.28 — Free Monthly Sky Map

The public/free Monthly sign preview now includes a restrained Luna wheel between **Where the sky is gathering** and **How the month unfolds**.

- The wheel is a geocentric tropical sky snapshot at 12:00 local time.
- If the selected month is current, the snapshot uses the current date in the report timezone.
- Future months anchor to the first day; past months anchor to the final day.
- The selected star sign is treated as whole-sign House 1, so the current planetary concentration is visibly mapped into sign-based houses.
- ASC/MC are deliberately omitted because a free sign forecast has no personal birth time/location.
- The paid Monthly remains differentiated by its natal overlay, recommendations, timing and downloadable personalised report.
- No existing Daily, Monthly, Natal, Solar Year, Stripe, email/PDF, astronomy-memory or evidence functionality was removed.
