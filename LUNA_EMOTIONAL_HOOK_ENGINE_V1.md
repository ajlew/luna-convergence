# Luna Emotional Hook Engine v1

The daily page now separates two editorial jobs:

```text
Emotional hook
-> wins the first five seconds

Interpretive theme
-> states the serious calculated meaning
```

The existing `headline` field remains the serious interpretive theme so
all prior narrative tests and evidence links remain stable.

New fields:

```python
hook_headline
hook_subline
tone_family
```

## Tone rotation

The engine rotates among:

- tongue-in-cheek;
- romantic tension;
- self-aware;
- boundary-led;
- dry practical humour;
- tender but direct.

The selected family is deterministic and uses:

- date;
- sign;
- strongest aspect;
- activated house pair.

There is no external AI call and no runtime cost.

## Grounding

Twelve house-specific hook banks provide the general fallback.
Important convergence pairs—such as Home × Relationships,
Communication × Romance, Career × Relationships and Money × Home—have
dedicated pair-specific hook banks.

## Guardrails

Every customer hook must:

- contain 4–14 words;
- contain no aspect, orb, house or retrograde jargon;
- avoid unsupported claims about another person's feelings;
- avoid guaranteed outcomes;
- remain different from the serious interpretive theme.

## Mobile hierarchy

The black hero now shows:

1. sign;
2. Today's Convergence;
3. emotional hook;
4. serious interpretive theme;
5. date.

The separate convergence block below Do / Don't was removed, reducing
visible repetition while keeping the convergence more prominent.
